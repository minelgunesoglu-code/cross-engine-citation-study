## Where the data comes from

The rows were written by hand on 19 June 2026 and on 6 and 7 August 2026 while
reading the answers of ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overview. Only
the brand names in each answer and the sources each engine displayed were
recorded. No answer text was copied, and no cited page was fetched, mirrored or
archived.
